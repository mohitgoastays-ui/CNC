Which formats are in scope — and why

In scope — real, accurate output from a heightfield:
STL · OBJ · PLY · 3MF · glTF (mesh) · SVG · DXF (vector/engraving) · depth PNG · G-code/NC. These derive directly from the depth surface, so they stay dimensionally honest.

Out of scope by design — would be fake conversions:
STEP · IGES · SAT · Parasolid are parametric B-rep solids; SLDPRT · IPT · CATPart · F3D · 3DXML · DWG · FBX are vendor-proprietary. A relief heightmap has no parametric feature tree, so producing these needs a full CAD kernel or the vendor's own software — forcing them would emit broken/meaningless files (exactly the overclaim we're avoiding).
