Before you cut. This G-code has not been validated on a physical machine. Run it in the air first (spindle off, Z raised), then in scrap, before committing good stock.

The program contains two tools and issues M6 between them. Many GRBL builds ignore M6 — if yours does, the finishing pass will run with the roughing cutter still fitted. Confirm how your controller handles the tool change, or export and run the two operations separately.

Work origin: X0 Y0 at the stock's front-left corner, Z0 at the stock top surface. All cuts are negative Z.
Which formats are in scope — and why

In scope: STL · OBJ · PLY · 3MF · glTF · SVG · DXF · depth PNG · G-code. All derive directly from the depth surface, so they stay dimensionally honest.

Out of scope by design: STEP · IGES · SAT · Parasolid are parametric B-rep solids; SLDPRT · IPT · CATPart · F3D · DWG · FBX are vendor-proprietary. A relief heightmap has no feature tree — emitting these would produce meaningless files.
